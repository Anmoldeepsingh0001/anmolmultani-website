How it works

What a pre-approval does for you.

  • Sets your budget: we review income, debts, credit and down payment so you know your real price range.
  • Holds a rate: most lenders hold a rate for a set period, protecting you if rates rise while you shop.
  • Spots issues early: credit or income questions get solved now, not in the middle of a deal.
  • Strengthens your offer: sellers and agents take pre-approved buyers more seriously.
Good to know

Make the most of it.

Do
  • Gather your documents early: ID, pay stubs, employment letter, two years of tax notices.
  • Keep your down payment where we can show its history.
  • Ask us before making any big financial change.
  • Talk to us again if your offer is close to your limit.
Avoid until closing
  • Taking on new credit (car loans, new cards, buy-now-pay-later).
  • Changing jobs or going on probation.
  • Moving large sums around without a paper trail.
  • Assuming a pre-approval is a final approval. The lender still reviews the property.

Questions

Pre-approval FAQ

Does a pre-approval hurt my credit?

A credit check is part of it and may cause a small, temporary dip. Several mortgage checks in a short window usually count as a single inquiry.

Is a pre-approval a guarantee?

No. It’s a strong indication. Final approval depends on the property, an appraisal if needed, and confirming your documents.

How long does it last?

Rate holds commonly run 90 to 120 days depending on the lender. If it runs out, we can usually renew it.

What if I’m self-employed or new to Canada?

There are lenders and programs for both. Check our guides, then let’s talk about your situation.
