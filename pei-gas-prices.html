Where today’s price comes from

How the forecast works

The forecast follows wholesale gasoline prices and the Canadian dollar through the same window IRAC averages, then applies IRAC’s published formula. It’s an estimate, not IRAC’s decision.

How PEI gas prices work

Everyone pays about the same. On PEI, gas prices are regulated by the Island Regulatory and Appeals Commission (IRAC). Every station has to price inside the same narrow band, which is why it’s the same in Charlottetown, Summerside and Souris.

Prices change twice a week. New prices take effect at 12:01 a.m. every Tuesday and Friday, so the change happens at midnight Monday and midnight Thursday. IRAC can also make an unscheduled change when markets move fast.

It starts with the wholesale “rack” price. IRAC takes the average wholesale price at the Charlottetown terminal over the last few days, then adds a fixed wholesale margin, any taxes, the station’s retail margin, and 15% HST.

That’s why it can be forecast. The wholesale price moves days before the pump does. Roughly, every 1¢ move in the wholesale average moves the pump price about 1.15¢.

Frequently asked questions

When do gas prices change in PEI?+

At 12:01 a.m. every Tuesday and Friday, set by IRAC. IRAC can also make an unscheduled change (an “interruption”) when the market moves sharply.

Why is gas the same price everywhere on the Island?+

IRAC sets a minimum and maximum price for every grade, and stations must stay inside that range. The gap is usually only about a cent.

How accurate is this forecast?+

It’s tested against IRAC’s actual prices. It’s usually within a few cents, and gets the direction right most of the time. Big world events and unscheduled changes can throw it off.

Where does the data come from?+

Official prices come from IRAC’s published petroleum price schedules. The forecast uses publicly reported wholesale gasoline futures and the Bank of Canada’s exchange rate. This page isn’t affiliated with IRAC or any fuel company.
