How it works

Is a refinance right for you?

  • Access equity: borrow up to 80% of your home’s value, minus what you owe.
  • Consolidate debt: replace high-interest balances with one lower-rate payment.
  • Lower your payment: extend your amortization (within lender limits) to ease cash flow.
  • Change structure: switch fixed and variable, add a line of credit, or remove a co-borrower.
  • Mid-term or at renewal: refinancing mid-term can mean a penalty. We calculate it first.

Questions

Refinance FAQ

How much equity can I access?

Usually up to 80% of your home’s appraised value, minus your current mortgage and any other secured debt.

Will I pay a penalty?

If you break a fixed term early, often yes: three months’ interest or an interest-rate differential. We’ll get the exact figure first.

Can I refinance with my current lender?

Yes, and sometimes that’s cheapest. We’ll compare it against switching.

How long does it take?

Typically two to four weeks, depending on appraisal and legal timing.
