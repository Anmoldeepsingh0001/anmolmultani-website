Privacy Policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

1. Who this policy covers

This policy explains how Anmol Multani, Mortgage Broker (“I”, “me”), handles personal information collected through anmolmultani.ca and in the course of arranging mortgages. I work with Clearstone Financial in Charlottetown, PEI, and mortgage brokerage services are provided through Mortgage Powered Financial Group Inc. (MPFG).

I handle personal information in line with Canada’s federal private-sector privacy law, the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

2. What I collect

  • Contact details you send through a form, email, phone, text or social media: name, email, phone number and your message.
  • Mortgage application information: income, employment, assets, debts, property details, identification and credit information. This is collected through the secure online application and documents you provide, not through this website’s forms.
  • Booking details when you schedule a call: name, email and the time you pick.
  • Technical information your browser sends automatically, such as IP address, browser type and pages requested. This is handled by the hosting and font providers listed below.

Please don’t send sensitive documents (ID, pay stubs, statements) through the contact or feedback forms. I’ll send you a secure way to share them.

3. How I use it

  • To answer your questions and give you mortgage options.
  • To prepare, submit and manage your mortgage application with lenders, mortgage default insurers and service providers involved in your file (for example appraisers and lawyers or notaries).
  • To verify your identity and keep records as required by law, including anti-money-laundering rules.
  • To check your credit, only with your consent.
  • To stay in touch about your mortgage, such as renewal reminders. You can opt out of non-essential messages at any time.

I don’t sell personal information.

4. Who it’s shared with

With your consent, information is shared with the people and organizations needed to arrange your mortgage: MPFG (my brokerage), lenders you apply with, mortgage default insurers, credit bureaus and the professionals on your file. MPFG and lenders handle your information under their own privacy policies too.

This website uses these outside services:

  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website.
  • FormSubmit: delivers contact, feedback and careers forms to my inbox.
  • Reclaim.ai: the booking calendar.
  • Online mortgage application platform: the “Start your application” button opens my brokerage’s secure application, which has its own privacy terms.
  • Google Fonts and cdnjs (Cloudflare): load fonts and page scripts.
  • YouTube: plays embedded videos when you press play.

Some of these providers store or process information outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be subject to local law.

5. Cookies and analytics

This site doesn’t use advertising cookies or tracking analytics. Your browser stores a few small settings on your own device, such as your light/dark theme choice. Outside services above (like embedded videos) may set their own cookies when you use them.

6. How long I keep it and how it’s protected

I keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above and for the record-keeping periods required by law and by my brokerage. Files are protected with access controls, secure systems and limited access on a need-to-know basis.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask to see the personal information I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or withdraw consent (which may affect what I can do for you). Email me and I’ll respond within 30 days.

8. Contact and complaints

Privacy questions or concerns: info@anmolmultani.ca. If you’re not satisfied with my answer, you can contact MPFG’s Principal Broker, Donna Thornton (donna@mpfg.ca), or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

9. Changes

I may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
