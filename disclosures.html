Disclosures & Complaints

Last updated November 27, 2026

1. Who I am

Anmol Multani, Mortgage Broker, based in Charlottetown, PEI, and working with Clearstone Financial. Mortgage brokerage services are provided through Mortgage Powered Financial Group Inc. (MPFG), FSRA brokerage licence #13720, 3310 South Service Road, Suite 303, Burlington, ON L7N 3M6. MPFG is a brokerage under Mortgage Intelligence.

Prince Edward Island doesn’t currently issue individual mortgage broker licences, so there’s no PEI licence number to show.

Nova Scotia clients are served by Nicholas Gaudet, licensed in Nova Scotia.

2. Where I can help

Mortgage services are only offered where the broker on your file is licensed. “Atlantic Canada” on this site describes where we’re based, not a licence in every province. If your property or situation is outside where we’re licensed, I’ll tell you and point you to someone who can help.

3. How I’m paid

In most cases I’m paid by the lender when your mortgage funds, so there’s no cost to you. Some lenders also pay brokerages volume-based bonuses. If a broker or lender fee applies to your mortgage (for example with some private or alternative lenders), I’ll disclose it in writing before you commit.

Before you sign, you’ll get the written disclosures required for your mortgage, including the lender, cost of borrowing, any fees and how I’m compensated.

4. Conflicts of interest and referrals

I recommend mortgages based on your needs, not on what pays me more. If there’s a relationship or payment that could affect a recommendation, I’ll tell you. If I refer you to another professional (such as a lawyer, insurance agent or accountant), I’ll let you know about any referral fee.

5. Smith Manoeuvre™

Smith Manoeuvre™ is a trademark used under licence through SMCG. It’s a debt-restructuring strategy that involves borrowing to invest and carries risk. Whether interest is tax-deductible depends on your circumstances. Get independent tax and legal advice before starting.

6. Rates, tools and website information

Rates are subject to change and lender approval (OAC). Calculators and tools give estimates only. See the Terms of Use.

7. Complaints

If something isn’t right, I want to hear about it.

  • Step 1, talk to me: email info@anmolmultani.ca with your name, what happened and what you’d like done. I’ll acknowledge it promptly and aim to resolve it quickly.
  • Step 2, my brokerage: if it isn’t resolved, contact MPFG’s Principal Broker, Donna Thornton, at donna@mpfg.ca or (905) 631-2854.
  • Step 3, the regulator: Nova Scotia clients can also contact Service Nova Scotia, which regulates mortgage brokers in that province.

8. Privacy

See the Privacy Policy for how personal information is handled.
