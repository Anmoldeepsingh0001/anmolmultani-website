Why Work With Us

Independent advice. A wide network. A modern process.

Whether you’re buying your next home or looking for a mortgage partner you can actually rely on for your own clients — here’s what sets working with me apart.

For clients

Why homebuyers work with me.

Independent

Not tied to one bank

I’m not a bank employee pushing one product. Through Vine Mortgage Group’s network I can shop your file across a wide range of lenders to find the right fit for your situation.

Access

A wide lender network

From the major banks to monoline and alternative lenders, I have options for straightforward files and complex ones alike — self-employed, newcomer, or otherwise.

Clarity

Plain-English, no pressure

You’ll always know what you qualify for and why. No jargon, no pushy sales tactics — just a clear plan and honest answers.

Speed

A modern, digital-first process

Free online calculators, plain-language guides, and a straightforward application process built to save you time at every step.

Reach

Atlantic Canada, and beyond

Based in Charlottetown and serving PEI, Nova Scotia, and New Brunswick directly, with Vine Group’s hub model extending reach across Canada.

Ongoing

A relationship, not a transaction

From your first pre-approval to your renewal years down the road, I keep track of your file and reach out when it’s time to revisit your rate.

For industry partners

Why partner with us.

If you’re a Realtor, financial advisor, accountant, or another professional whose clients need mortgage financing, here’s what a referral partnership looks like.

Process

A seamless referral system

Send a referral and stay in the loop — clear updates as the file moves through pre-approval to closing, without you having to chase for status.

Technology

Industry-leading tools

Digital calculators, plain-language guides, and a modern application process mean your clients get a faster, clearer experience than the traditional back-and-forth.

Trust

Your client relationship stays yours

I’m here to support the mortgage piece of the puzzle — not to compete for the relationship you’ve built with your client.

Who this works well for

Realtors who want financing sorted quickly so deals close on time. Financial advisors and accountants whose clients’ mortgage decisions affect their broader financial picture. Insurance professionals, lawyers, and notaries who regularly cross paths with home financing. If your clients ever ask “do you know a good mortgage broker?” — let’s talk.

Start a partnership conversation

For planning and discussion only. Not legal, tax, or accounting advice.
