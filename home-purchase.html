How it works

We do the heavy lifting.

  • Compare lenders: banks, credit unions and monoline lenders, side by side.
  • Structure the deal: down payment, amortization, fixed or variable, prepayment options.
  • Protect your offer: help setting financing conditions and timelines with your realtor.
  • Clear conditions: we gather what the lender needs so you hit your deadlines.
  • Close smoothly: we coordinate with your lawyer through closing day.
Good to know

Buying smart.

Plan for
  • Closing costs of roughly 1.5% to 4% of the price.
  • Mortgage default insurance if you put down less than 20%.
  • A home inspection and, sometimes, an appraisal.
  • PEI first-time buyer programs if you qualify.
Watch out for
  • Making an offer without a financing condition.
  • Spending your closing-cost cushion on furniture early.
  • Only comparing rate. Penalties and flexibility matter too.
  • Big credit changes between approval and closing.

Questions

Home purchase FAQ

How much do I need for a down payment?

At least 5% on the first $500,000 and 10% on the portion above that, up to $1.5 million. Homes $1.5 million and up need 20%.

Fixed or variable?

Fixed keeps your payment steady. Variable moves with prime and usually has lower break costs. We’ll compare both for your plans.

How long does financing take after an offer?

Most financing conditions are 5 to 10 business days. Having a pre-approval in place makes it faster.

Are there programs for first-time buyers in PEI?

Yes. See our PEI first-time buyer programs guide.
