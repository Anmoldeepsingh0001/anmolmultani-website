How it works

Your road home, mapped out.

Six clear stops from first call to keys in hand, and we’re with you after that too.

  1. 01

    Free call

    15 minutes on your goals, timeline and numbers. No pressure, no credit check yet.

  2. 02

    Pre-approval

    We review your income and credit, then hold a rate so you can shop with confidence.

  3. 03

    Shop & offer

    You find the place. We help you write an offer with the right financing conditions.

  4. 04

    Lender approval

    We compare lenders, submit your file and clear the lender’s conditions.

  5. 05

    Closing day

    Your lawyer finalizes everything and the keys are yours.

  6. 06

    Ongoing check-ins

    Renewal reviews and equity check-ups, so your mortgage keeps working for you.
