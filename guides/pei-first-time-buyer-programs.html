What each program actually gives you

Down Payment Assistance Program

Up to $17,500 5% of price Income ≤ $110,000 Price ≤ $350,000 10-yr term

A second loan behind your mortgage, run by Finance PEI. Interest accrues at 5%/year but is forgiven once you’ve repaid the principal in full — goes toward your down payment only.

Closing Cost Support Program

A direct contribution toward legal fees and other closing costs — separate application, separate money, on top of the DPAP. First-come, first-served until the province’s budget is committed.

Who qualifies for what

The Down Payment Assistance Program and Closing Cost Support Program share the same core test: a first-time buyer (haven’t owned in the last 4 years, or been through a relationship breakdown), household income of $110,000/year or less, a home priced at $350,000 or less in PEI, owner-occupied as your principal residence with no rental portion, satisfactory credit, and Canadian citizenship or permanent residency. The land transfer tax exemption runs on its own test: 6 months’ PEI residency (or 2 of the last 6 tax years filed), first-time buyer, principal residence — no income or price cap.

How they stack together

These aren’t either/or. A qualifying buyer can use the DPAP toward their down payment, the Closing Cost Support Program toward legal and inspection fees, and pay zero land transfer tax on the same purchase — then layer on the federal FHSA and RRSP Home Buyers’ Plan covered in our First Time Home Buyer Guide.

Frequently asked questions

Can I combine the Down Payment Assistance Program, the Closing Cost Support Program, and the land transfer tax exemption?+

Yes. They’re three separate programs with their own applications, and eligible buyers can use all three on the same purchase, alongside federal help like the FHSA and RRSP Home Buyers’ Plan.

How much can PEI’s Down Payment Assistance Program give me?+

A conditionally interest-free loan of up to 5% of your purchase price, to a maximum of $17,500. It’s repayable over 10 years, and the accrued interest is forgiven once you’ve repaid the principal in full. Your household income must be $110,000 or less and the home $350,000 or less.

What does the Closing Cost Support Program cover?+

A contribution of up to $2,500 toward the closing costs on your first home purchase in PEI, for buyers with household income of $110,000 or less on a home priced at $350,000 or less.

Do I automatically get the PEI land transfer tax exemption as a first-time buyer?+

No — you need to meet the province’s residency and first-time buyer criteria and file a signed declaration form at closing. Your lawyer or notary handles this as part of registering the deed.

Where do I apply for these PEI programs?+

The Down Payment Assistance Program and Closing Cost Support Program are administered by Finance PEI and PEI Housing Corporation. We can point you to the right application and tell you which ones you’re likely to qualify for based on your file.
