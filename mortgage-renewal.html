How it works

Renew on your terms.

  • Start early: many lenders will hold a rate up to 120 days before your maturity date.
  • We shop it: we compare your bank’s offer with lenders across our network.
  • Switch or stay: if your bank matches, great. If not, we move you to a better lender.
  • Rethink the setup: renewal is a good time to change term, payment frequency or amortization.
Good to know

Renewal tips.

Good to do
  • Mark your maturity date and call us four months before.
  • Check whether your needs changed: income, family, plans to move.
  • Consider a shorter term if you might sell or refinance soon.
  • Ask about prepayment privileges, not just the rate.
Common mistakes
  • Signing the first renewal letter that arrives.
  • Letting it auto-renew into a high posted rate.
  • Waiting until the last week, when options are limited.
  • Assuming switching lenders is expensive. Often it isn’t.

Questions

Renewal FAQ

Can I switch lenders at renewal without a penalty?

Yes. At maturity your term is over, so there’s no prepayment penalty to move.

Will I have to re-qualify?

Staying with your lender usually doesn’t require it. A straight switch to a new lender may not require the stress test, but they’ll still review your file.

Who pays switching costs?

Many lenders cover appraisal and legal costs on a straight switch. We’ll confirm before you decide.

When should I start?

About four months before your maturity date. That gives time to hold a rate and compare.

Got your renewal letter?

Send it over. We’ll tell you within a day if you can do better.
