How it works

Flexible borrowing, done right.

  • Borrow as needed: draw funds anytime up to your limit, then repay and reuse.
  • Pay interest only on what you use: an unused line costs nothing in interest.
  • Variable rate: usually prime plus a margin, so payments move with rates.
  • Combined with your mortgage: many lenders pair a HELOC with your mortgage in one readvanceable plan.
Good to know

Use it well.

Great for
  • Renovations and repairs.
  • An emergency fund you hope not to use.
  • Investing, including the Smith Manoeuvre™.
  • Bridging between buying and selling.
Keep in mind
  • Interest-only payments don’t reduce the balance.
  • Rates are variable and can rise.
  • It’s secured by your home.
  • You must qualify on income and credit.

Questions

HELOC FAQ

How much can I get?

Up to 65% of your home’s value as a line of credit, and up to 80% combined with your mortgage, minus what you owe.

What’s the rate?

Usually prime plus a margin, set by the lender. It moves when prime changes.

HELOC or refinance?

A HELOC is flexible and reusable. A refinance gives a lump sum at a usually lower, possibly fixed rate. We’ll compare.

Can I get one if I’m 55+ and on a fixed income?

If income is tight, a reverse mortgage may be a better fit. See our reverse mortgage page.
